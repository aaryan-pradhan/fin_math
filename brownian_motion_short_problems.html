Brownian Motion Short Problems

A five-question BM assignment with printed answers. Each problem is followed by its solution; the source gives only final answers, and the working here is added. Source: BM assig with answer.pdf.

Problem 1 — Four Wiener-process quantities

Problem. Let $\{W(t),t\ge0\}$ be a Wiener (standard BM) process. Find (a) $E\left((W(t)-W(s))^2\right)$; (b) $E(W(s)\mid W(t)=x)$ for $0<s<t$; (c) $\operatorname{Cov}(W(s),W(t))$ for $0<s<t$; (d) $E(W(t)\mid W(s)=x)$ for $0<s<t$.
Solution.
  1. $W(t)-W(s)\sim N(0,t-s)$, so the answer is $\mathbf{t-s}$.
  2. Brownian bridge: $(W(s)\mid W(t)=x)\sim N\!\left(\tfrac stx,\ \tfrac{s(t-s)}t\right)$, so the answer is $\mathbf{sx/t}$.
  3. $\operatorname{Cov}(W(s),W(s)+(W(t)-W(s)))=\operatorname{Var}W(s)=\mathbf s$.
  4. $E(W(s)+(W(t)-W(s))\mid W(s)=x)=x+0=\mathbf x$.

Problem 2 — Mean of GBM

Problem. Let $\{Y(t),t\ge0\}$ be a geometric Brownian motion with $Y(0)=a$. Find $E(Y(t))$.
Solution. $Y(t)=ae^{X(t)}$, where $X$ is a BM with drift coefficient $\mu$ and variance parameter $\sigma^2$, so $X(t)\sim N(\mu t,\sigma^2t)$. By the normal MGF, $$E(Y(t))=a\,e^{\mu t+\sigma^2t/2}=\mathbf{a\,e^{t(\mu+\sigma^2/2)}}.$$

Problem 3 — Numerical GBM mean

Problem. $\{Y(t)\}$ is a GBM with drift parameter $\mu=0.01$ and volatility $\sigma=0.2$. If $Y(0)=100$, find $E[Y(10)]$.
Solution. By Problem 2: $100\,e^{10(0.01+0.02)}=100e^{0.3}\approx\mathbf{134.99}$. The source prints $134.98$, a rounding difference.

Problem 4 — Random walk → BM with drift

Problem. A random walk moves up or down $\sqrt{\Delta t}$ in each time step $\Delta t$, with probabilities $p$ and $1-p$, where $p=\tfrac12(1+\mu\sqrt{\Delta t})$. Argue that as $\Delta t\to0$ the limit is a Brownian motion with drift rate $\mu$.
Solution. The source says "See class notes". The argument is the same as in Brownian Motion Additional Problems, Problem 8(a). With $X(t)=\sqrt{\Delta t}\sum_{i\le t/\Delta t}X_i$, $X_i=\pm1$: $$EX_i=2p-1=\mu\sqrt{\Delta t},\qquad\operatorname{Var}X_i=1-\mu^2\Delta t,$$ so $EX(t)\to\mu t$ and $\operatorname{Var}X(t)\to t$. The CLT gives normal limits, and independent steps give independent increments.

Problem 5 — Conditional distribution of BM with drift

Problem. Let $\{X(t)\}$ be a BM with drift coefficient $\mu$ and variance parameter $\sigma^2$. What is the conditional distribution of $X(t)$ given $X(s)=c$ when (i) $s<t$; (ii) $t<s$?
Solution. Write $X(t)=\sigma B(t)+\mu t$.
  1. $s<t$: $X(t)=c+[X(t)-X(s)]$, with the increment independent and $N(\mu(t-s),\sigma^2(t-s))$: $$[X(t)\mid X(s)=c]\sim N\big(c+\mu(t-s),\ \sigma^2(t-s)\big).$$
  2. $t<s$: $X(s)=c$ means $B(s)=(c-\mu s)/\sigma$. By the bridge, $B(t)\mid B(s)\sim N\big(\tfrac tsB(s),\tfrac{t(s-t)}s\big)$, so $$E[X(t)\mid X(s)=c]=\mu t+\sigma\frac ts\cdot\frac{c-\mu s}\sigma=\frac{tc}s,\qquad[X(t)\mid X(s)=c]\sim N\!\left(\frac{tc}s,\ \frac{\sigma^2t(s-t)}s\right).$$
The source answer to (ii) prints the mean as $\mu t+\sigma tc/s$. The drift cancels: once the endpoint $X(s)=c$ is fixed, the mean is $tc/s$, whatever $\mu$ is. Check with $c=0$: the path is pinned at $0$ at both ends, so the mean must be $0$, but the source formula gives $\mu t$. The variance $\sigma^2t(s-t)/s$ in the source is correct.
